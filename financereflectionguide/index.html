Lower Monthly Payments with End Flexibility

Personal Contract Purchase (PCP)

PCP gives you lower monthly payments by delaying a large portion of the vehicle's cost (the "Balloon Payment") until the very end of the agreement.

Estimated Monthly
£278.45
48 fixed payments
30-Second Summary of PCP

You do not pay for the whole car during the 48 months. You only pay for what the car is expected to lose in value (depreciation), plus interest. At the end, you decide whether to pay the remaining balloon to keep it, trade it in, or simply hand it back.

Your 3 Clear Options at the End of PCP:

A Option 1: Keep the Car

Pay the final optional balloon payment (£9,350.00) plus any option fee, and you become the 100% legal owner. You can also refinance this balloon amount if preferred.

B Option 2: Part-Exchange

Trade the car in for another car. If the trade-in valuation is higher than the balloon payment, the remaining equity acts as your deposit towards your next vehicle.

C Option 3: Return the Car

Return the car to the finance company with nothing more to pay, provided you have stayed within your 10,000 annual miles and good condition standards.

Mileage & Condition Obligations:

Your agreed limit is 10,000 miles per year. Exceeding this incurs a charge per mile if you return the car. BVRLA Fair Wear & Tear standards apply on vehicle return.

Direct Path to 100% Outright Ownership

Hire Purchase (HP)

HP is simple and straightforward. You pay equal monthly installments that spread the total cost of the vehicle. When the final payment is made, you own the vehicle outright.

Fixed Monthly
£465.12
48 equal installments
30-Second Summary of HP

You pay off the full price of the vehicle across equal monthly installments. There is no large final balloon payment and there are no mileage penalties. Once the last payment clears, the car is 100% yours.

Pure Long-Term Rental / Lease

Personal Contract Hire (PCH)

PCH is like a long-term car rental. You pay fixed monthly rentals to use the car, and return it at the end. You never own the car and have no option to buy it.

Monthly Rental
£315.00
VAT inclusive rental
30-Second Summary of PCH

You pay to use the vehicle for a set time (e.g. 48 months). You never build equity and cannot purchase the vehicle at the end. You return the vehicle and can take out a brand new lease.

Your Take-Home Reflection & Discussion Checklist

Before making a final choice, review these 4 key questions at home with someone you trust.

1

1. Realistic Monthly Affordability

Can you comfortably afford the monthly payment alongside household utility bills, council tax, groceries, and insurance, with extra room for unexpected expenses?

2

2. Realistic Mileage Forecast

Does your agreed annual limit (10,000 miles) match your daily commute, holidays, and family visits? Excess mileage incurs fees if the car is returned.

3

3. Involve a Trusted Support Person

Would you like to discuss this sheet with a friend or family member? You are always welcome to bring a support person or nominated representative back to the dealership with you.

4

4. Free Independent Advice Access

Free, confidential, and impartial guidance on borrowing and household budgeting is available from MoneyHelper.org.uk (backed by HM Government) or Citizens Advice.
