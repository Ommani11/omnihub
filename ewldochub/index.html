Personal Contract Hire (PCH)

Finance Product Declaration

EW Leasing exclusively offers Contract Hire Finance agreements, which are fixed-term rental arrangements where you hire the vehicle and will never own it. By proceeding, you acknowledge your commitment to these specific financial obligations and vehicle care responsibilities. Please review the key terms below to ensure this rental product meets your needs.

Key Information

  • Contract Hire is purely a rental agreement, and you will never own the vehicle or have the option to buy it.
  • All rental payments attract VAT, and your regular payment amounts will change if the VAT rate is adjusted.
  • You must maintain the vehicle and return it in a condition that meets the standards in the BVRLA Guidelines.
  • You will be required to pay additional financial charges if you exceed your agreed annual mileage limit.
  • Ending the contract early incurs termination fees, and missing payments can negatively impact your credit score.
